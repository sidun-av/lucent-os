Dock-taskbar

Centred, floating 14 px above the edge, as wide as its content. Launcher, search, pinned and running apps, then a compact tray and the clock. All in one glass bar.

Every running window stays reachable: a dot per window, hover or click shows them all, and AltTab still works. Search is WinSpace. Window buttons stay on the right.
